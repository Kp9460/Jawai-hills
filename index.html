CHAPTER 00 — THE PREMISE

Jawai is the only place in India where leopards walk past shepherds and nobody runs. No fences. No cages. Just granite, water, and an old agreement between man and cat. This page is your journal — stamp it as you go.

01 / THE HILLS

Granite that
remembers paws.

Bera, Devgiri, Kala Pahar — billion-year-old rocks hollowed into caves. Leopards den inside, sun themselves on top. Because the land is open, you don't search for the cat. You read the hill — and the hill tells you.

↳ field tip: look for langurs alarm-calling. where monkeys shout, the spotted one sits.

02 / THE WATER

A dam full of
wings & teeth.

Rajasthan's largest dam holds mugger crocodiles, flamingos, pelicans and 100+ migratory birds. Morning safari ends here — mist on water, chai in hand, eagles overhead.

03 / THE PEOPLE

Trackers who
read dust.

Rabari herders graze sheep in leopard country every day — and lose almost none. They track on foot at 4 AM and radio your gypsy. You ride. They read. The cat decides.

↳ "leopard ko hum dekhte nahi, pehchante hain." — Ramesh Rabari, tracker since 2009
